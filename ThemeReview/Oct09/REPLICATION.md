## Data limits

GPU statistics are displayed as unavailable unless configured with a real custom provider. Bluetooth shows the actual number of connected devices; no battery or invented microphone values are shown. Workspace count follows the real desktop count. Thin source bars are made taller to keep 14pt text legible.
